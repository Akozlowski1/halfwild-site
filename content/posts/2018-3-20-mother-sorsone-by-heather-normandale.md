<p><em>"Mother Sorsorne was inspired by a beautiful balafon melody from a West Guinean song called sörsörnë - from the Baga People's tradition. We worked it into an original song titled Mother about our human dependence on the earth.</em><em> We cannot survive without this vast and beautiful planet. The original lyrics to the traditional Sörsörnë song are sung in the Baga language and sing about about daughters thanking their mothers for giving them the tools for life.</em><em>"</em> - <em>Heather Normandale Band</em></p><p><strong>Mother Sorsorne</strong> by <em>Heather Normandale</em><br><br>The caterpillar inches through the grass<br>Finds her niche, never questioning this path<br>Moving open from the ground into the sky<br>Find your place, find your place, find your place, find your place<br>Find your place, find your place, find your place, find your place ahh</p><p>The breath upon your mothers lips makes the rivers run makes the mountains lift<br>The love that moves you to exist begins with a thunder a moan and a hiss</p><p>Sometimes i feel like a baby<br>Clung to the worlds aching breast<br>I feel her hands softly guide me<br>Mother i try to do my best<br>Mother i try to do my best</p><p>Standing in front of the brilliant sun even with your eyes you can feel it run<br>From the top of your face it melts into the eyes<br>And the world with a grin she takes off her disguise<br><br>Sometimes i feel like a baby<br>Clung to the world's aching breast<br>I feel her hands softly guide me<br>Mother i try to do my best<br>Mother i try to do my best<br>Mother i try to do my best</p><p>(Mother Sorsone has been released as a single <a href="https://heathernormandale.bandcamp.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.)</p>
