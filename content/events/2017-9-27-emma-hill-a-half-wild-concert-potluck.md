<p>Half Wild welcomes Emma Hill and Bryan Daste to Oakland!</p><p><a href="/">Half Wild</a> is a community of artists and music appreciators deeply connected to nature. </p><p>"Alaskan singer/songwriter Emma Hill and her co-writer/musician Bryan Daste have quickly made a massive impact with their alt/folk songs and their penchant for touching upon the deepest, most universal of subjects. They’ve been compared to Gillian Welch and David Rawlings, and their fourth album proves they’re not just a one-off, fly-by-night duo. Hill easily exudes that wild, adventurous style that only a true Alaskan can..."<br>- TOM H A L L E T T- Elmore Magazine</p><p>Listen to Emma here: <a href="http://www.emmahillmusic.com/listen/">http://www.emmahillmusic.com/listen/</a></p><p>For our concert/potluck together, please bring food to share! Also, we would encourage a $10-20 donation for the artists.</p><p>THANK YOU TO OAKLAND OASIS HOUSE FOR HOSTING!</p>
