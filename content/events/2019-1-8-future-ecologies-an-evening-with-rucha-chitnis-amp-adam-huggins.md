<p>Half Wild is thrilled to present <strong>Future Ecologies: An Evening with Rucha Chitnis and Adam Huggins</strong>.</p><p>Join us for an evening of community and conversation. Please bring a dish or snacks to share. We will begin with nourishments at 6:30. Please be sure to arrive no later than 7:30, as our live interview begins at 7:45.</p><p>On this special evening Adam Huggins will interview photojournalist and documentary filmmaker Rucha Chitnis live for the Future Ecologies podcast. Our interview will follow a screening of Rucha's award-winning documentary short, "<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Z0EpUVr7Gg" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>In the Land of My Ancestors</strong></a>," which celebrates the living legacy of Ann Marie Sayers, a beloved Ohlone elder. Rucha's film is the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/InTheLandOfMyAncestors/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>2018 Platinum Winner</strong></a> of the International Independent Film Awards.</p><p>In <a href="http://www.futureecologies.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Future Ecologies</strong></a>' first season, Adam Huggins and co-founder Mendel Skulski have displayed a remarkable talent for weaving stories, interviews, music, and soundscapes into a highly engaging podcast on par with East Coast analogs Inside/Out and Here be Monsters. Listen to <a href="https://www.futureecologies.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Season 1 of Future Ecologies podcast</strong></a>.<br><br>As <a href="https://www.awomanslens.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Rucha Chitnis</strong></a> articulates, "Women are our invisible life force. They are the world's farmers, seed savers, peace builders, human rights defenders and environmental guardians. As a photojournalist, I am passionate about making the invisible visible--particularly women's counter narratives and grounded alternatives in the face of human rights abuses, food insecurity and ecological and climate crises."<br><br>-- Thanks to our friends at The Lion's Den for welcoming us into their home! --</p>
