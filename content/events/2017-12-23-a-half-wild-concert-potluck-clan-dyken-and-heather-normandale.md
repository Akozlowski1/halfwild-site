<p><a href="/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Half Wild</a> is excited to welcome <a href="http://www.clandyken.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Clan Dyken</a> and <a href="http://www.heathernormandale.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Heather Normandale</a>!<br><br>--- <a href="/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Half Wild</a> brings together artists and music appreciators grounded in a deep respect for nature, to speak, through song, to that wild part of each of us. The goal of Half Wild is cultural change to return nature to our collective consciousness. ---<br><br>For our concert/potluck together, please bring food to share!
