<h1><strong>Half Wild</strong> is a Pacific Coast initiative featuring writers, scientists, filmmakers, musicians, and artists who translate the Pacific Rim’s living systems — from Baja to Alaska, and inland where ocean climate shapes forest, river, and desert — into felt experience. Through essays, soundscapes, film, music, and gatherings, we illuminate what is beautiful, strange, and alive, celebrating creative expression and ecological understanding as twin paths to deeper connection and care.</h1>
