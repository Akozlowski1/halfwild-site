<h1>EDITORIAL CIRCLE</h1><p><em>The Half Wild Editorial Circle is a small group of practitioners who help steward the voice, values, and curatorial integrity of the Field Journal. Members bring lived relationship to place, disciplined creative practice, and shared commitment to ecological literacy, care, and formal quality. The circle supports editorial discernment, ongoing conversation about direction and form, and contribution by example through members’ own work.</em></p>
<h4>Andrzej Kozlowski</h4>
<p>Andrzej Kozlowski is a songwriter, soundscape recordist, and philanthropic advisor based in Portland, Oregon. He curates songs, stories, and gatherings that bring ecology, art, and conversation into relationship. Alongside his creative practice, he has spent two decades working in international philanthropy, and his writing appears in Alliance Magazine, PEAK Insight Journal, and Exponent Philanthropy.</p>
<h4>Stephen Lezak</h4>
<p>Stephen Lezak is a writer and researcher whose work grows from long attention to northern landscapes and the lived realities of climate change. He works at the intersection of climate politics and environmental justice, with a particular interest in how awe, story, and perception shape our relationship to a warming world. <a href="https://www.stephenlezak.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">His writing</a> appears in publications including Emergence, The New York Times, The New Republic, and High Country News. He lives in Berkeley, California.</p>
